# Support verification during drafting

On September 13, 2026, the user added verification during drafting to LexCerta's scope: Claude or ChatGPT must be able to call LexCerta when the Customer asks it to create a draft, as well as when reviewing a supplied draft, and each pilot lawyer must exercise both workflows. The accepted drafting workflow checks proposed citations and quotations, revises or flags unresolved material, and requires a final whole-draft review tied to the delivered draft, accompanied by a summary and PDF report that clearly identify any incomplete checking. The AI host creates the document and LexCerta provides evidence under the existing source, retention, and verification-claim boundaries; one drafting request groups its intermediate checks and final review under at most one credit within published workload limits and the accepted completion conditions.
